FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How accurate is the AI disease detection?

Our AI model is trained on thousands of crop images and boasts over 95% accuracy in identifying common diseases. However, we always recommend consulting a local expert for confirmation.

2. How do I sell my produce on the marketplace?

Simply go to the "Sell" tab, click "Add Product", upload photos of your harvest, set the price and quantity, and publish. Buyers can then contact you directly.

3. Is AgriLink free to use?

Yes, the basic features including disease detection and community access are free. We charge a small commission only when you successfully sell a product on the marketplace.

4. Can I use the app without internet?

Yes, you can log your farm data and view past analytics offline. The app will sync your data to the cloud once you reconnect to the internet.

5. How do payments work?

We use a secure escrow system. The buyer pays AgriLink, and the funds are released to you once the buyer confirms they have received the produce in good condition.
